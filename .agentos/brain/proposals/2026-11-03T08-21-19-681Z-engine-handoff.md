## Drift is expected

Any commit after a checkpoint is saved, including a commit of the checkpoint itself, makes that checkpoint show as `drifted`. This is expected and is not a failure; a committed checkpoint does not stay current. The next engine runs `agentos resume --task <id>`, inspects current files, re-runs the stale checks and saves a new checkpoint.
